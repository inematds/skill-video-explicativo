How it works in 1 minute

01

You provide a topic

Claude writes the script in 6–9 scenes, generates the narration in PT-BR with local TTS (Kokoro), and measures the actual durations.

02

Turns into animated HTML

A generator builds each scene in HTML + GSAP, with audio and animation timed by the same array of timestamps. Premium dark, amber accent.

03

Renders to MP4

HyperFrames runs headless Chrome + FFmpeg and exports the video in 16:9 (YouTube) and 9:16 (Shorts/Reels). No cloud, no API.

Download the Skill explainer video
